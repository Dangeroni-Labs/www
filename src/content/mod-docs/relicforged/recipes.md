Start with Resonant Alloy for the first new tool tier. Later, craft a Relic Forge, a forging template and the Blackened parts you need.

## Resonant tools

Use the familiar vanilla recipes for a Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe and Sword, with Resonant Alloy in place of the usual material.

At the Relic Forge, combine a Relic Forging Template, the matching Diamond tool, a Blackened Handle and its matching head. The Blackened Sword uses a Blackened Edge instead of a head.
