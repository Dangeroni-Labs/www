Kossman Stats is a modern rework of GokiStats, bringing persistent character progression to modern Minecraft.

Upgrade stats using XP to improve gathering, combat, defense, movement, survival, and loot-related abilities.

## Features

- Up to 50 levels per stat
- Persistent per-player progression
- Stat downgrades with a 70% XP refund
- Partial stat loss on death
- Mining, digging, chopping, and trimming progression
- Swordsmanship, bowmanship, and pugilism
- Defensive, survival, and movement stats
- Treasure Finder and Mining Magician mechanics
- Configurable balance and gameplay settings
- Custom stat interface with inactive, active, and max-level states

This is the first port-focused release. Future updates will introduce more Kossman Stats-specific mechanics, balance changes, UI improvements, and support for additional loaders.
