Relicforged adds new tool progression built around vanilla materials, custom forging and unique tool abilities.

The current beta includes Resonant tools, Blackened tools, the Relic Forge and Searing.

## Current progression

Iron → Resonant → Diamond → Blackened

More progression is planned for later releases.
